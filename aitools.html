🔬 Google NotebookLM
Module 1 · Research Assistant · notebooklm.google.com
Analogy: NotebookLM is like hiring a brilliant graduate research assistant who reads only the sources you assign — not the entire internet. Every answer it gives traces back to your uploaded documents, with citations. No hallucinations from unknown sources; no off-topic tangents.

What Makes NotebookLM Different

Most AI chatbots draw from their general training data — which means they can confidently state things that aren't in your course materials. NotebookLM is grounded exclusively in your uploaded sources. Ask it a question, and it answers using only what you gave it, with page-level citations. This makes it uniquely trustworthy for academic work.

Powered by Google Gemini 3 (as of late 2025), NotebookLM now also generates slide decks, infographics, mind maps, and full podcast-style audio discussions directly from your sources.

Typical Faculty Uses

📖 Literature Reviews

Upload 20 journal articles. Ask "What are the three main debates in this literature?" NotebookLM synthesizes across all sources with citations.

🎓 Lecture Preparation

Upload your course readings. Ask "Generate five discussion questions based on Chapters 3 and 4." Saves hours of prep time.

📋 Study Guides

Upload a textbook chapter. Generate a formatted student study guide with key terms, summaries, and review questions — all grounded in the text.

🎙️ Audio Overviews

NotebookLM's Audio Overview converts your sources into a realistic two-host podcast discussion — a compelling review format students can listen to while commuting.

📝 Quiz Generation

Upload an article and prompt: "Create 10 multiple-choice questions at the Apply and Analyze levels of Bloom's Taxonomy."

🗂️ Research Synthesis

Upload competing scholarly sources and ask: "What are the areas of agreement and disagreement across these authors?" Instant cross-source synthesis.

👺 Goblin.Tools

Analogy: Goblin.Tools is like a Swiss Army knife for your brain. Each mini-tool is a different blade — a task-splitter, a tone-checker, a time-estimator, a brain-dump organizer. You don't use all of them at once, but when you need one, it's exactly the right shape.

What Is Goblin.Tools?

Created by developer Bram De Buyser, Goblin.Tools is a free suite of AI micro-tools designed specifically for people who find large, vague tasks overwhelming — originally built with ADHD and neurodivergent users in mind, but universally useful for anyone managing a complex workload.

The web version is completely free and ad-free. A mobile app is available for approximately $2 (one-time purchase — no subscription). App sales fund the free web version.

🎨 Napkin AI
Module 3 · Visual Diagramming · napkin.ai
Analogy: Napkin AI is like having a graphic designer sitting next to you who can read your mind. You write a paragraph explaining a concept — Napkin instantly hands you three polished diagram options for that exact content. You pick the one that fits. No shapes, no connectors, no formatting — just your idea, visualized.

What Napkin AI Does — and Doesn't Do

Napkin AI does one thing exceptionally well: it converts structured text into clean, presentation-ready diagrams in under ten seconds. It's not a full presentation builder (that's Gamma). It's not a mind mapper (that's Mapify). It's the tool you reach for when you need to turn a written explanation into a visual — fast.

As of 2025, Napkin has grown to over 5 million registered users, making it one of the fastest-adopted AI design tools in education and business.

🔁 Flowcharts

Process descriptions become step-by-step flow diagrams. Paste "steps in the scientific method" and receive three flowchart variants.

🗺️ Concept Maps

Explanatory text becomes node-and-connection diagrams showing relationships between ideas — perfect for showing how concepts relate in a discipline.

📊 Process Diagrams

Cyclical or linear processes — like the water cycle, legislative process, or cell division — visualized from a paragraph of text.

🏢 Org Charts

Describe a hierarchy in text; receive a clean organizational chart with labeled nodes and levels.

📤 Export Options

Export diagrams as SVG (editable in Illustrator or Canva), PNG (for slides), or directly to PowerPoint. Free tier includes basic exports.

🤝 Collaboration

Share visual documents with colleagues. Paid plans include team collaboration and custom branding for institutional consistency.

🗺️ Mapify
Module 4 · AI Mind Mapping · mapify.so
Analogy: Mapify is like a skimmer reader who can distill any source — a 3-hour YouTube lecture, a 50-page PDF, a podcast episode — into a structured visual map of the key ideas in about 30 seconds. You get the skeleton of knowledge; you bring the depth.

What Is Mapify?

Formerly known as Chatmind, Mapify (relaunched 2024–2025 from Edinburgh, Scotland) uses AI to convert nearly any content type into an interactive, expandable mind map. Unlike manual mind mapping tools, you provide the source — Mapify does the intellectual work of identifying structure, hierarchy, and key relationships.

It's especially popular with students for summarizing academic texts and with faculty for course planning and curriculum mapping.

Supported Input Types

📄 PDFs & Documents

Upload research articles, textbook chapters, or syllabi. Mapify identifies the structure and generates a hierarchical mind map with key terms and concepts.

▶️ YouTube Videos

Paste a YouTube URL. Mapify reads the transcript and generates a visual map of the lecture content — with timestamped navigation so you can jump to any section.

🌐 Websites

Paste any URL and Mapify maps the page content. Useful for mapping competitor syllabi, policy documents, or research organization websites.

🎙️ Audio & Podcasts

Upload audio files or provide podcast links. Mapify transcribes and maps the content — useful for summarizing recorded lectures or conference talks. (Pro feature.)

⚡ Gamma
Module 5 · Presentations, Websites & Docs · gamma.app
Analogy: Building a presentation in PowerPoint is like writing an essay with scissors and glue — tedious, error-prone, and time-consuming. Gamma is like dictating your ideas to a professional designer who returns a polished, structured deck in 60 seconds. You focus on the thinking; Gamma handles the design.

What Is Gamma?

Gamma is the world's most-used AI presentation platform, with over 250 million presentations, websites, and documents generated to date. In September 2025, Gamma 3.0 introduced the world's first AI design agent — a conversational partner that can restyle entire decks, rewrite content, or adjust tone with simple chat commands.

Unlike PowerPoint, Gamma generates card-based visual content that works as both a self-readable document and a live presentation — no reformatting needed when switching between modes.

🎯 Presentations

Type a prompt → get a full slide deck with text, images, speaker notes, and professional layouts in 30–60 seconds. Export to PowerPoint for final edits.

🌐 Websites

Generate a shareable course overview website, a project showcase, or a resource hub from a simple text description. No coding required.

📄 Documents

Create visual reports, syllabi overviews, and policy briefs that are readable as documents and presentable as slides — same content, two modes.

🤖 Gamma Agent

The Gamma 3.0 AI agent redesigns, rewrites, or restructures your entire deck from a chat command: "Make this more concise" or "Add a darker color scheme."

🖼️ AI Images

Gamma selects and generates relevant images for each slide based on content — eliminating the stock photo hunt that consumes hours of presentation prep.

📤 Export

Export to PowerPoint (.pptx) for submission or further customization. Shareable via web link for async student review — no software required to view.

📅 Histography
Module 6 · Historical Timeline Visualization · histography.io
Analogy: Histography is like Google Maps for time. Just as you can zoom from a continent down to a street view, Histography lets you zoom from all of human history down to a single decade — and click any event to read its Wikipedia entry. It makes the density of history visible in a way no textbook can.

What Is Histography?

Histography is a free, web-based interactive timeline visualization tool that displays millions of historical events sourced from Wikipedia. The timeline is zoomable — you can view 14 billion years of history or zoom into a single decade. Every dot on the timeline is a real event you can click to explore.

It's a discovery tool rather than a creation tool — faculty use it to provide historical context, spark student curiosity, and build timeline-based learning activities.

🔍 Zoomable Timeline

Pan and zoom from the Big Bang to the present day. The density of events in any era becomes immediately visible — revealing patterns you can't see in a linear textbook.

📂 Category Filters

Filter events by category: Wars, Science, Art, Politics, Religion, Music, Sports, etc. Study how events across categories cluster in the same historical moment.

🔗 Wikipedia Integration

Every event links directly to its Wikipedia article. Students can begin exploring a timeline cluster and drill down into primary sources and further reading.

📱 Visual Density

The number of dots in a time period reflects how many documented events occurred. Visually comparing the documentation density across eras prompts critical thinking about historical record bias.

Supported Disciplines

📜 History & Political Science

Show how the Civil Rights Movement, Cold War events, and decolonization overlapped in the same decades. Context becomes visible.

🎭 Art & Music History

Compare artistic movements to concurrent political and social events — watch Impressionism emerge during the Franco-Prussian War era.

🔬 Science History

Trace the timeline of scientific discoveries and see how they cluster — the explosion of physics from 1895–1935, for example.

📊 Sociology

Trace social movements, legislation, and population shifts on the same visual timeline to show systemic change over time.

🧪 Faculty Application

Timeline-Based Learning Activity

  • Zoom to 1760–1850 and observe the Industrial Revolution alongside political upheaval
  • Zoom to your discipline's most significant historical moment
🎙️ Fish Audio
Module 7 · Voice Generation & Cloning · fish.audio
Analogy: Fish Audio is like having a recording studio and a voice actor on call 24 hours a day. You write a script at midnight; your AI voice narrates it with natural cadence and emotion. Need it in Japanese? Spanish? Arabic? Done. You never have to re-record — just edit the text.

What Is Fish Audio?

Fish Audio is an advanced AI voice generation and voice cloning platform that produces ultra-realistic speech in 40+ languages. It's described as a cost-effective alternative to ElevenLabs — offering comparable quality at roughly half the price.

The voice cloning capability is particularly powerful: provide just 10–30 seconds of clear audio, and Fish Audio trains a voice model in under one minute. That cloned voice can then read any text in 40+ languages.

🗣️ Text-to-Speech

Generate natural-sounding narration from any text. Dozens of catalog voices across genders, accents, and tones. Minimal latency — production-quality output.

🔬 Voice Cloning

Upload 10–30 seconds of your own voice (or any voice with permission). Fish Audio creates a reusable voice model — clone it once, use it forever for any text.

🌍 Multilingual

Generate audio in 40+ languages including Japanese, French, Arabic, Spanish, German, and more — all with native-level quality from the same cloned voice.

♿ Accessibility

Convert lecture notes, syllabi, and readings into high-quality audio versions. Students with visual impairments or reading disabilities gain equal access to all course content.

🎭 Character Voices

Historical reenactments, case study characters, or persona-based simulations. Give a case study protagonist a consistent voice that students recognize throughout a course.

🎓 Course Narration

Narrate PowerPoint slides, LMS modules, or video content without recording yourself. Update narration by editing text — no re-recording required.

🧠 Recall
Module 8 · Personal Knowledge Management · getrecall.ai
Analogy: Recall is your personal encyclopedia — but one that grows automatically. Every article you read, video you watch, and podcast you listen to gets added to a living knowledge base that you can search, chat with, and query. It's the difference between a pile of browser bookmarks you never revisit and a searchable second brain that remembers everything.

What Is Recall?

Recall (getrecall.ai) is an AI-powered personal knowledge management system. Save any online content in one click — articles, YouTube videos, PDFs, podcasts, TikToks, Google Docs. Recall automatically summarizes each item, organizes it, links it to related content in a knowledge graph, and lets you chat with everything you've collected using the AI model of your choice (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and others).

It received $1.5M in seed funding (led by VC Jason Calacanis) after a viral Hacker News post — and now offers a free-forever tier with no credit card required.

🔖 One-Click Saving

Browser extension for Chrome and Firefox. Click the Recall button on any webpage, YouTube video, or article — saved, summarized, and categorized automatically.

📝 AI Summaries

Every saved item receives an automatic AI-generated summary. Quickly scan your library without re-reading full articles. Summaries are linked to the original for verification.

🕸️ Knowledge Graph

Recall automatically identifies connections between saved items — showing you how a journal article, a YouTube lecture, and a news story are conceptually linked, even if you saved them months apart.

💬 Chat with Your Library

Ask questions across your entire saved knowledge base: "What have I saved about formative assessment?" or "Summarize everything I've collected on climate policy this month."

🔁 Spaced Repetition

Recall resurfaces saved content using spaced repetition algorithms — the same science behind flashcard apps — to help you actually retain what you've read.

📱 Cross-Platform

Web app, Chrome extension, Firefox add-on, iOS app, and Android app. Your knowledge base is accessible everywhere, including mobile while traveling.
