Module 1

Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

From concept to classroom relevance

📚 7 lessons

🎯 Learning Objective

Define artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning, and explain their growing role in education and the workforce.

💡 Why This Matters

AI is transforming every sector of the economy and every level of education. Understanding the fundamentals — not just how to use the tools, but how they actually work and why they matter — positions you to make informed decisions, guide students thoughtfully, and lead institutional change instead of reacting to it.

👥 How This Fits Your Role

📚 Instructors

Contextualize AI for your students with confidence. Answer the foundational questions they're already asking — and help them separate genuine capability from media hype.

🔬 Professors

Ground your teaching and research in a rigorous understanding of how AI systems work and where they fail — an essential foundation for any serious academic engagement with the topic.

🏛️ Administrators

Build the foundational literacy needed to lead informed conversations about AI adoption, workforce alignment, and the institutional changes AI will drive across your campus.
