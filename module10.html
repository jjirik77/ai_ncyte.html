Module 10

Ethics & AI Policies

Using AI responsibly in the classroom

📚 5 lessons

🎯 Learning Objective

  1. Develop a classroom AI Acceptable Use Policy grounded in ethical principles, bias awareness, academic integrity standards, and data privacy requirements.

💡 Why This Matters

Your students are already using AI — with or without a policy in place. Educators who establish clear, evidence-based expectations protect their students, their institutions, and their own professional standing. This module equips you to act before problems arise, not after.

👥 How This Fits Your Role

📚 Instructors

Set clear AI use expectations before confusion — or violations — occur. Your Acceptable Use Policy is your first and most important classroom governance tool.

🔬 Professors

Navigate academic integrity with consistency and confidence. Understand what AI detection tools can and cannot do — and why that matters for how you grade and evaluate student work.

🏛️ Administrators

Build institutional AI policies that satisfy accreditation requirements, data privacy law, and student equity principles — before being caught without one when a case arises.
