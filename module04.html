Module 4

Other Useful AI Tools

Beyond the AI assistant — specialized AI for educators.

🛠️ 6 tools

🎯 Learning Objective

  1. Evaluate five specialized AI tools — Napkin.ai, NotebookLM, Mapify, Gamma, and Goblin Tools — for classroom applicability, and demonstrate practical use in at least two educational contexts.

💡 Why This Matters

ChatGPT and Copilot are just the beginning. A growing ecosystem of specialized AI tools can save you hours every week — turning a lecture outline into a visual diagram, converting reading notes into a podcast, or breaking a complex assignment into manageable student steps. This module introduces five high-value tools that educators across all disciplines can use immediately.

👥 How This Fits Your Role

📚 Instructors

Turn your course materials into polished presentations with Gamma, visualize concepts with Napkin.ai, and help students organize knowledge with Mapify mind maps.

🔬 Researchers

Use NotebookLM to synthesize literature, generate study guides from PDFs, and create AI-powered conversations with your own uploaded sources.

🎓 Students

Goblin Tools helps neurodiverse and all learners break overwhelming tasks into smaller, manageable steps — a practical accessibility and productivity tool.
