Goal
What This Guide Covers

To help teachers generate visually appealing, content-rich handouts using ChatGPT's advanced graphic generation capabilities, based on a detailed lesson outline. In this example, the handout will compare types of business ownership.

💡 Key Insight

The new AI models can generate textbook-quality graphical content as if you had your own graphic designer. This works for any subject — science, history, math, ELA, or any career and technical field.

Step 1
Prepare the Lesson Content

Use your detailed lesson outline about your topic, or refer to the following structure for a business class on Types of Business Ownership:

  • Sole Proprietorship
  • Partnership
  • Corporation (C-Corp, S-Corp)
  • LLC
  • Cooperative
  • Franchise
  • Key comparison points (e.g., liability, taxation, ease of formation)
💡 Pro Tip

The more specific your content outline, the better the AI result. List your main concepts, key terms, and the comparison dimensions you want visualized before writing your prompt.

Step 2
Open ChatGPT & Use the Prompt

In ChatGPT, use the following prompt to generate a visual handout. Copy and adapt this prompt for your own subject and content:

Act as an instructional designer and advanced graphic generator. Create a high-quality, visually engaging handout for students in a business class. The topic is "Comparison of Types of Business Ownership in the U.S." Generate a handout design that includes: - A clear title and subtitle - A visual comparison chart or infographic comparing Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Cooperative, and Franchise - Key categories: Liability, Taxes, Formation Difficulty, Ownership, Example Businesses - Clean, modern formatting suitable for print or PDF - Business-relevant icons or illustrations to enhance visual appeal - Use bold headings, section dividers, and enough white space for readability Make this handout look like it belongs in a college-level business course.
💡 Customization Tip

Replace the topic, comparison items, and categories with your own subject. The structure of this prompt works for any discipline — just swap out the content details.

Step 3
Review the Generated Handout

ChatGPT will return a photo-quality handout image (usually in PNG format). Review the image for these criteria before downloading:

Accuracy of the content
Clarity of layout and visual organization
Proper comparison categories
Readability — can students quickly interpret it?
If Refinement Is Needed

Use follow-up prompts to improve the result:

  • "Please revise the graphic to simplify the text"
  • "Add icons for each business type to make it more engaging"
Steps 4–5
Download, Use & Create Variations
Step 4 — Download and Use the Graphic
  • Click the download icon on the generated image
  • Save the file as PNG or JPEG
  • Insert the image into Google Docs or Word handouts
  • Use in PDF worksheets, Google Slides, or PowerPoint
  • Embed in your LMS (Canvas, Blackboard, etc.)
  • Print for in-class distribution
Step 5 — Ask for Additional Variations

Use follow-up prompts to create multiple materials from the same outline:

  • "Now make a poster-sized version of the handout."
  • "Create a worksheet version with fill-in-the-blank definitions for each business type."
  • "Make a black-and-white version for easy printing."
  • "Add quiz questions based on the content of the handout."
Bonus Tips
Bonus Tips for Teachers
✅
Accuracy Check
Always check AI-generated visuals for factual accuracy. AI can produce confident-looking graphics that contain subtle errors — review content before distributing to students.
👥
Student Engagement
Consider having students interpret the handout as a warm-up or group activity — "What do you notice? What do you wonder?" works well with any visual.
📚
Cross-Curricular
You can use this method to make visuals for any subject — science, history, math, or ELA. The prompt structure works across disciplines. Just swap the content.
